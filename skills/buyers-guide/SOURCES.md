# Sources — buyers-guide skill

These authoritative, Australian, beginner-focused sources were distilled (via
`book-to-skill`) into this skill. They are mature/government or consumer-org
sources. Processes and figures change — prefer the live official pages over any
hardcoded detail.

- **Moneysmart (ASIC)** — Buying a car (budgeting, loans, running costs):
  https://moneysmart.gov.au/buying-a-car
- **ACCC** — New and second-hand cars (consumer guarantees, rights, PPSR):
  https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/specific-products-and-activities/new-and-second-hand-cars
- **NSW Government** — Buying a used vehicle:
  https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/buying-and-selling-vehicles/buying-a-used-vehicle
- **NSW Government** — Vehicle inspections checklist:
  https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/buying-and-selling-vehicles/buying-a-used-vehicle/vehicle-inspections-checklist
- **PPSR** — Personal Property Securities Register (money owing / stolen / write-off):
  https://www.ppsr.gov.au/

Note: state-specific rules (statutory warranties, cooling-off, transfer windows)
differ by state/territory; check your state's consumer-protection agency for
local detail.
