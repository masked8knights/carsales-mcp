# 04 · Rego, PPSR & write-off checks

Do these **before** paying a deposit. They verify the car's identity and that it's
yours to buy cleanly.

## PPSR — Personal Property Securities Register
- Tells you if there is **money owing** on the car (still under finance), or if it's
  recorded as **stolen or written off**.
- Run a PPSR check online (ppsr.gov.au) or by phone (1300 007 777).
- Keep the **search certificate** — request it (and the contract of sale) from the seller.

## Written-off status
- A write-off (e.g. after a major crash or flood) can be recorded nationally from 2004.
- A written-off car may be unsafe or uneconomic to repair — treat as a red flag and
  investigate the category before proceeding.

## Registration check (state)
- Free/low-cost state rego check confirms the rego is current/suspended/cancelled and
  any restrictions. (NSW: free registration check + paid vehicle history report covering
  write-off, stolen, odometer readings, past use e.g. taxi/hire car.)

## VIN / identity
- The **VIN (or chassis number)** and **engine number** on the car must match the
  paperwork and the listing. Mismatches = walk away.

## Transferring registration
- After buying, you typically have a limited window to transfer rego into your name
  (e.g. **14 days in NSW**) and pay transfer fees. Organise CTP/green slip first.
- Interstate or unregistered cars need a safety/identity inspection (e.g. NSW AUVIS)
  before registration.
