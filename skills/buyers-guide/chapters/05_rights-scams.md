# 05 · Rights & scams

## Consumer guarantees (Australian Consumer Law)
Apply to cars bought from a **licensed dealer** (new and used). Automatic rights that
**cannot be excluded** by a business:
- acceptable quality (safe, durable, free of defects, given age/price/condition)
- fit for any disclosed purpose
- match description / demo model
- clear title, no undisclosed securities, undisturbed possession.

If a dealer car fails a guarantee, you may get a **repair, replacement, or refund**
(major failures = refund/replacement). Extended warranties are *additional* and cannot
remove these guarantees.

## State statutory warranties
- Most dealer-sold used cars are covered by a **state/territory statutory warranty**
  (varies by state; often tied to age/km — e.g. under ~10 years / 160,000 km).
- **Cooling-off periods** may apply for dealer sales but are **not** in every state.

## Private & auction sales
- **Almost no ACL protection.** You rely on your own judgement.
- Always do a **PPSR check** (money owing) and verify ownership yourself.
- NSW Fair Trading cannot resolve private-sale disputes — recourse means legal action.

## Common scams / red flags
- Prices **far below market** for the make/model.
- Seller refuses inspection, won't meet, or pressures quick payment.
- Unauthenticated online-marketplace listings (prefer trusted platforms).
- "No refunds" signs or claims that try to waive your basic rights — **illegal**.
- Mismatched VIN/rego/odometer vs paperwork or listing.

## If something goes wrong
- Go back to the **dealer** first (they must assist, not redirect you to the manufacturer).
- Keep records. Escalate to your state/territory consumer protection agency; report
  misconduct to the ACCC.
