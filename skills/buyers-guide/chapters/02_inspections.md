# 02 · Inspections

A thorough inspection catches problems before you own them. Combine this with the
**car-inspection** skill, which directs the vision model to assess the listing photos.

## Walk-around (body & accident signs)
- **Panel gaps even?** Uneven gaps between panels can mean prior accident repairs.
- **Colour match between panels?** A panel a different shade suggests repaint.
- **Overspray** on door rubbers, plastic trim, or windows = hidden repaired damage.
- Dents, rust (especially sills, arches, subframe, floor), and mismatched panels.

## Under the car & engine bay
- Leaks, corrosion on terminals, missing/bodged parts.
- Water or rust under the mats / spare-wheel well = possible past flood or poor repair.
- Check the **spare tyre, jack, and tools** are present.

## Inside
- Rips/stains, warning lights on the dash, worn pedals inconsistent with the odometer,
  aftermarket wiring.

## Test drive (varied roads)
- Cold start, acceleration, braking pull, steering wander, unusual noises.
- Confirm the seller's insurance will cover you as a driver.

## Pre-purchase inspection (PPI)
- Strongly consider a **licensed mechanic / independent inspector** — the best defence
  against a lemon.
- Request the **service history** and any recent inspection reports.

## Cross-check the documents
- VIN / chassis number, engine number, and rego plate on the car must match the
  paperwork and the listing.
- Odometer in photos should match the stated odometer.
