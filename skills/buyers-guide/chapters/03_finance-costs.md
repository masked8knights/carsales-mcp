# 03 · Finance & running costs

## Buying with a loan
- Read and understand the **finance agreement** — it comes with the contract of sale.
- Compare **total repaid**, not just the monthly payment. Watch for balloon payments
  and early-exit fees.
- Dealer finance is convenient but not always cheapest — get outside quotes.

## Total cost of ownership (budget for these)
- **Registration** (annual, state-based)
- **CTP / green slip** — compulsory third-party personal-injury insurance (required to
  register); in NSW bought as a "green slip" before registration.
- **Comprehensive insurance** — quote it before you buy; can be surprisingly high.
- **Fuel / charging**, **maintenance & repairs**, **tyres**.
- Loan **interest**.

## Insurance
- Get a quote on the specific make/model/postcode **before** committing.
- Understand excess, agreed vs market value, and what's excluded.

## Practical tip
A cheap purchase price can become expensive once rego, insurance, and a loan are added.
Model the **all-in monthly cost** and make sure it fits the budget from chapter 01.
