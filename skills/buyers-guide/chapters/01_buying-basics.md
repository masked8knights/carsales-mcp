# 01 · Buying basics

## Set a budget first
- Include the **total cost of ownership**, not just the price: registration, CTP/green
  slip, insurance, fuel, maintenance/repairs, and loan interest.
- Get an **insurance quote before** committing — premiums vary a lot by age, model, and
  postcode.
- If financing, the loan is separate from the car price — compare the **interest rate
  and total repaid**, not just the monthly amount.

## Where to buy (and what it means for your rights)
| Channel | Warranty / protection | Test drive | Risk |
|---------|----------------------|------------|------|
| **Licensed dealer** | ACL consumer guarantees + state **statutory warranty** + usually a **cooling-off period** | Yes | Lowest |
| **Auction** | None, and usually **no test drive** beforehand | No | Higher |
| **Private sale** | **Almost no protection** (ACL guarantees generally don't apply) | Maybe | Highest |

- Prefer **trusted platforms** (e.g. carsales) over unauthenticated marketplaces where
  scams are more common.
- At a dealership, **read the contract of sale carefully before signing** — it's legally
  binding. Check the finance agreement is included and understood.

## Steps
1. Decide needs vs wants (body style, seats, fuel type, EV charging access).
2. Set a hard budget including running costs.
3. Shortlist via `search_all_cars` (carsales + Facebook).
4. Vet each candidate with `get_listing_details` + the **car-inspection** skill.
5. Verify history (chapter 04) and rights (chapter 05) before any deposit.
